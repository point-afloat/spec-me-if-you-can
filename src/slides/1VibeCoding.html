Vibe Coding

The term was coined by Andrej Karpathy (next slide).

…fully give in to the vibes,
embrace exponentials,
and forget that the code even exists.

- Andrej Karpathy, February 2025

The quote has the animated gradient from the start. The post that coined "vibe coding": Andrej Karpathy on X, 2 February 2025 (https://x.com/karpathy/status/1886192184808149383, link taken from the Wikipedia article's footnote). Excerpt of the post, verified against the Wikipedia article's quote; the post continues with the first words "There's a new kind of coding I call vibe coding, where you ...". He describes accepting all changes without reading the diffs and pasting errors back in, which he calls fine for throwaway weekend projects. Worth saying aloud: the original meaning was explicitly for throwaway projects.

Why does it go wrong?

The intent only lives in the chat.

Nothing to check against.
How do we know it's done?

Nothing protects what already works.
